Our Story

Family-Run,
Heartfully Made

Mother Fast Food & Restaurant is one of Surat's oldest and most beloved Gujarati food providers. Since 1998, we have been dedicated to serving authentic vegetarian Gujarati cuisine with an unwavering commitment to quality, taste, and hygiene.

As a family-run kitchen, we handle everything ourselves — no external staff — ensuring that each dish meets the same high standard our loyal customers have cherished for over two decades.
